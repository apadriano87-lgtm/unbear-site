FAQ

When does $UNBEAR launch?
Thursday 8 October 2026 at 20:00 Warsaw time (CEST, UTC+2), which is 18:00 UTC, on par.family on Robinhood Chain.
What's the contract address?
It isn't public yet. At launch it is published only on unbear.fun (this site) and t.me/unbear_fun. @unbear_token on X only reposts the same address, so always compare it with this site. Any "$UNBEAR" address you see before then is fake.
Where does the 1.5% fee go?
The 1.5% total fee on each trade is the par.family base fee of 1% (half to the par.family protocol, half to the creator) plus a 0.5% creator tax that is set at launch and can't be changed. So 0.5% goes to the par.family protocol and 1% goes to $UNBEAR. That 1% is split 50/50: 0.5% of every trade funds the Weekly bear kick (a manual buyback and burn every week, logged with tx links above), and 0.5% goes to the public project wallet for promotion and meme contest prizes.
Is the dev buy locked?
Yes. The dev buy is 0.02 ETH (≈14.3M tokens, ~1.43% of the 1B supply after the 1.5% fee), and the tokens are locked in Sablier for 90 days with a gradual unlock. The Sablier link is posted at launch. The supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 with no creator allocation and no mint.
Where is the liquidity?
All liquidity sits in one Uniswap v4 ETH pool on par.family, locked permanently by par.family. There are no other pairs.
Will the price go up?
Nobody knows, and we don't promise anything. $UNBEAR is a memecoin: it has no intrinsic value and can go to zero. Buybacks and burns are not a promise about price.
Will someone from the team DM me?
No. We never DM first, never ask for your seed phrase, and never ask you to connect your wallet on another site. Anyone doing that is an impersonator.
